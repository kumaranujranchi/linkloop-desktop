Frequently Asked Questions

What is a collaborative link exchange?
A collaborative link exchange is an arrangement where two website owners place links on their websites pointing to each other's websites (direct exchange) or arrange a 3-way/indirect exchange (A links to B, B links to C, C links to A) to safely boost overall search visibility. LinkBuild provides a safe marketplace to negotiate and monitor these exchanges.
How does LinkBuild ensure safety?
We verify every website submitted to the platform using automated domain lookup checks. Additionally, we enforce spam score limitations and track each user's "Reputation Score" based on their response rates, completion rates, and historical trust badges.
What happens if my partner removes a backlink?
Our automated Backlink Monitor checks all completed exchanges daily. If a link is removed, changed to a nofollow relation, or redirected, our system immediately flag-alerts you and penalizes the offending site owner's reputation score.
