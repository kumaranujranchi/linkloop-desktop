Users may not upload, publish, distribute, or display content that:

Users are solely responsible for the content they submit.

3. COPYRIGHT INFRINGEMENT NOTICE

If you believe that content hosted on LinkBuild infringes your copyright, you may submit a Copyright Takedown Notice containing the following information:

Required Information

  • Your full legal name.
  • Your company name (if applicable).
  • Contact email address.
  • Contact phone number (optional).
  • Description of the copyrighted work.
  • Exact URL of the allegedly infringing content.
  • Statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is unauthorized.
  • Statement that the information provided is accurate.
  • Statement that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
  • Electronic or physical signature.

4. SUBMITTING A COPYRIGHT COMPLAINT

Copyright complaints may be submitted to:

Email: legal@linkbuild.store

Subject Line: Copyright Takedown Request

We recommend including all supporting documentation to facilitate review.

5. REVIEW PROCESS

Upon receiving a valid copyright complaint, LinkBuild may:

  • Review the complaint.
  • Request additional information.
  • Temporarily disable content.
  • Remove content.
  • Restrict visibility.
  • Suspend accounts.
  • Terminate accounts for repeat violations.

LinkBuild reserves the right to determine whether action is appropriate.

6. COUNTER-NOTIFICATION

If your content has been removed due to a copyright complaint and you believe the removal was made in error, you may submit a Counter-Notification containing:

  • Your full legal name.
  • Contact information.
  • Identification of removed content.
  • Explanation of why removal was incorrect.
  • Statement under penalty of perjury that the content was removed by mistake.
  • Consent to applicable legal jurisdiction where required.
  • Electronic signature.

Counter-notifications should be sent to:

Email: legal@linkbuild.store

Subject: Copyright Counter-Notice
