DOMAIN VERIFICATION POLICY

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

This Domain Verification Policy explains how LinkBuild ("LinkBuild", "Platform", "we", "our", "us") verifies website ownership and how verification badges should be interpreted by users of the marketplace.

By listing a website on LinkBuild or interacting with verified domains, you acknowledge and agree to this Policy.

1. PURPOSE OF DOMAIN VERIFICATION

The purpose of domain verification is to provide reasonable evidence that a user has control over, ownership of, or authorization to manage a website listed on the LinkBuild marketplace.

Domain verification is intended solely as an ownership verification mechanism.

Verification is not intended to certify website quality, trustworthiness, SEO performance, traffic accuracy, or business legitimacy.

2. WHAT A VERIFIED DOMAIN MEANS

A "Verified Domain" badge indicates that LinkBuild has successfully confirmed that the listing owner had control over the domain at the time verification was completed.

Verification may be completed through methods such as:

  • DNS verification
  • HTML file verification
  • Meta tag verification
  • Email verification
  • Other ownership verification methods approved by LinkBuild

The verification badge only indicates successful completion of the verification process.

3. WHAT A VERIFIED DOMAIN DOES NOT MEAN

A verified badge does NOT mean that LinkBuild has:

  • Audited the website
  • Approved the website
  • Endorsed the website
  • Guaranteed website quality
  • Guaranteed backlink quality
  • Verified traffic claims
  • Verified SEO metrics
  • Verified revenue claims
  • Verified domain authority scores
  • Verified domain rating scores
  • Verified business legitimacy
  • Verified future ownership
  • Verified compliance with search engine guidelines

Users should not rely solely on a verification badge when making business decisions.

4. NO GUARANTEE OF WEBSITE QUALITY

LinkBuild does not evaluate or guarantee:

  • Organic traffic quality
  • Search engine rankings
  • Domain Authority (DA)
  • Domain Rating (DR)
  • Spam Score
  • Trust Flow
  • Citation Flow
  • Indexation status
  • Backlink profile quality
  • Content quality

Users are solely responsible for conducting independent due diligence.

5. OWNERSHIP MAY CHANGE

Domain ownership and control may change over time.

A domain that was verified at one point may:

  • Be sold
  • Change ownership
  • Change management
  • Expire
  • Be transferred
  • Be repurposed

LinkBuild does not continuously guarantee ownership status after verification has been completed.

6. USER RESPONSIBILITY

Before entering into any backlink exchange, guest post agreement, sponsorship arrangement, payment transaction, or other business relationship, users should independently verify:

  • Domain ownership
  • Website quality
  • Traffic quality
  • SEO metrics
  • Website reputation
  • Business legitimacy

Users should not rely solely on platform badges, metrics, or representations.
