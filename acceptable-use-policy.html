This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") governs the use of LinkBuild ("Platform", "Service", "Website", "we", "our", "us"). By accessing or using LinkBuild, you agree to comply with this Policy.

Users must use LinkBuild lawfully, honestly, and responsibly.

You may only list websites that:

  • You own, or

  • Impersonate website owners.
  • Submit fake ownership claims.
  • Misrepresent authority over a website.
